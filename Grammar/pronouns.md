# Pronouns

The currently decided Pronouns are given below:

Pronoun | Swarnami | Derived from
| --- | --- | ---
I | Ne | Dravidian {Naan/Nenu/Nyaan/Nanu}
We | He (pronounced heh, not hee) | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Ham}
You | Te | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Tum}
You - Plural or Formal [optional use] | Nihe | Dravidian {eg. Tamil - Neenga}
She | Une | Austroasiatic {Santali - Uni}
He | Ane | Austroasiatic {Santali - Uni}
It/This/That | Ve | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Vah}
Respect / They | Ke | Tibeto-Burman {Khasi - Ki}

Please feel free to give feedback on these decided pronouns.
