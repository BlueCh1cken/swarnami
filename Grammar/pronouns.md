# Pronouns

The pronouns follow one rule. They end with the suffix -e (IPA Pronounciation: e) Borrowed from all language families to have equality in this core part of the language. 

The currently decided Pronouns are given below:

Pronoun | Swarnami | Derived from
| --- | --- | ---
I | Ne | Dravidian {Naan/Nenu/Nyaan/Nanu}
We | He (pronounced heh, not hee) | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Ham}
You | Te | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Tum}
You - Plural or Respect [optional use] | Niihe | Dravidian {eg. Tamil - Neenga}
She | Une | Austroasiatic {Santali - Uni}
He | Ane | Austroasiatic {Santali - Uni}
It/This/That | Ve | Indo-Aryan {eg. Hindi - Vah}
Respect / They | Ke | Tibeto-Burman {Khasi - Ki}

Note: For male 3rd person pronouns, both Ane and Ke can be used based on which version you are comfortable with (as Indian languages sometimes do not have gendered 3rd person pronouns). For 3rd person female pronouns, both Une and Ke can be used. The expected outcome is regional differences of the usage of these words, depending on existing language rules.

Please feel free to give feedback on these decided pronouns. After a roughly stable period, the pronouns will be locked and exceptional justification for changes will be needed.
