# Swarnami

## Goal

A neutral, inclusive and easy to learn & use language for communication across India, whilst preserving the Indic identity.

---

## Key Principles

- Never a 1st language (never making local languages obselete)
- Maximum regularity
- Maximum phonetic consistency
- Maximum ease of learning for all Indian speakers
- Community Driven
- Bharati Script
- Changes happen slowly

---

# Key Rules

## Nouns

- Genderless, for eg. in English:
  - Man
  - Woman
  - Teacher
  - Book

Suffixes are not gendered.

---

## Plurals

- A unified suffix for all plurals.

e.g.

- -an
- -hu
- -ne
- etc. (Yet to be picked)

---

## Verbs

- No conjugation. Subject is inferred from pronoun.

---

## Word Order

### English

I eat food

- subject
- verb
- object

### Hindi

Main khana khate hoon

- subject
- object
- verb

### Tamil

Naan saapadu saapidugiren

- subject
- object
- verb

- Most Indian languages use Subj.-Obj.-Verb.

---

## Word Quotas

- max. 50%, min. 30% Indo-European Roots.
- max. 40%, min. 20% Dravidian.
- max. 15%, min. 5% Sino-Tibetan.
- max. 15%, min. 5% Austroasiatic.
- max. 10%, min. 5% Shared.

---

## Word Rules

*(from ↑ to ↓ priority)*

1. Popularity /10
2. Ease of understanding /10
3. Familiarity /10

The Popularity score must be 6/10 or more, Ease score should be 5/10 or more, and the Familiarity score should be 4/10 or more for a word to be approved into the language.
