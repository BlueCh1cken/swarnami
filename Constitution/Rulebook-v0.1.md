# Rulebook: Swarnami
[v1.0.0]

## 1. Core Values
- Most ease of understanding & learning
- Most regularity (Both phonetically & gramatically)
- Community based development
- Preserving national and regional identities
- Non-replacing of local languages
- Gradual development

## 2. Representation
Swarnami has tried to fairly represent all languages, while still taking into account the population of the langugage family's speakers.
The below framework has been designed is by no means perfect, but it does make give all languages an opportunity to be involved in Swarnami, without forgetting that some language families have more speakers than others. This is optimised for usability, and *may* change in the future.

The amount of vocabulary borrowed from each of these families are as follows:
 - Indo-Aryan: 30-50%
 - Dravidian: 20-40%
 - Tibeto-Burman: 5-15%
 - Austroasiatic: 5-15%
 - Shared: 5-10%

## 3. Governance
The protocol will evolve as more loopholes are found, but so far there is no real penalty for breaching any of the values or principles of the language, or violating code of conduct. Depending on behaviour, it may lead to bans. Since we are trying to make this a very open place, there will not be any action taken against violating the rules of the actual language making, but violations may simply get a reminder and edits to changes.

To add major changes, the following protocol must be followed:
1. Large scale, public discussions must take place
2. Communtiy review of the language must take place
3. Moderator reviews must take place
4. Changes are added

For minor changes:
1. Smaller public discussions are encouraged
2. Community review is encouraged
3. Changes are added
4. If issues are flagged, changes may be removed.

The descision of what change is major or minor is up to contributors so far.

## 5. Amendments
Formal (or informal, we don't mind) proposals must be sent to (at least) one of the moderators. They shall discuss with the other moderators to decide of changes in the Rulebook.
